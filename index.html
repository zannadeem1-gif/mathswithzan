What is the online work schedule?

Maths requires a lot of practice in order to do better in the exams. However telling teenagers - or anyone - to do x amount of work by the end of the week or month is not the most effective or enjoyable way to get it done. So I have developed a system, where after each lesson, and as I get to know each student, we get super specific about what exactly they need to do to master each mini topic or exam question.

This becomes a daily task, around 15 minutes (may be longer for year 11s) that is easy to do but also super effective.

Its like a super awesome work out or diet plan that actually works.

Every 2 - 4 weeks there is a visible and measurable improvement in your child's maths ability.
